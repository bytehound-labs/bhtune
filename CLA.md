> agreement. It has not yet had a legal review. No contribution is bound by it until: (1) it

## Why this exists

BHTune is released under the [GNU AGPL v3.0-or-later](LICENSE). This CLA records the rights
needed to accept and maintain contributions and does not change the project's public license.

## Signing (not yet active)

Once finalized, this will be enforced by a CLA-assistant bot that comments on your first pull
request with a link to sign electronically. No mailed paperwork should be required for individual
contributors. Corporate contributors (contributing on behalf of an employer) will need a
corporate CLA variant naming both entities — not yet drafted.
